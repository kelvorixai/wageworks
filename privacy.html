Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. Plain-language summary: this site doesn't ask you to sign up for anything, and the only outside party involved is the ad network that may eventually fund the site.

What we collect directly

WageWorks has no accounts, no sign-up forms, and no newsletter box on any page. There's nothing to enter and nothing that's saved or sent to a server as you read.

Cookies and advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar device identifiers to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. This is standard for ad-supported publishing and is not something we control directly — it's covered by Google's own policies, not a separate tracking system we've built.

You can see and adjust how Google personalizes ads for you at adssettings.google.com, and read more about how Google's advertising partners use data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If no ads are currently running on a given page, this section describes what happens once they are — this policy is written to stay accurate as that rolls out, not just for the site's current state.

Analytics

If we add basic, aggregate traffic analytics in the future (for example, to see which guides are useful), this section will be updated to name the tool and what it collects before that happens — nothing beyond what's described above is running today.

Who this site is for

WageWorks is general-audience content and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect any information from children, which is easy to be confident about, since we don't collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes — a new analytics or ad provider, for instance — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

This project is run pseudonymously. A dedicated contact address will be added here once the site's permanent domain and account are set up; until then, this page itself is the disclosure of record.

This policy is provided for transparency and general compliance purposes and is not a substitute for legal advice.
