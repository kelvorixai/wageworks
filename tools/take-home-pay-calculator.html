How this is calculated

This follows the component breakdown from our basic vs. gross salary guide:

  • Housing + transport + other allowances: gross salary minus basic salary
  • Basic-to-gross ratio: basic salary ÷ gross salary — the figure that determines your gratuity accrual, since UAE end-of-service gratuity is calculated from basic salary, not gross
  • Take-home pay: gross salary minus any monthly deductions (loan repayments, pension contributions for UAE/GCC nationals, salary advances)

This is a planning estimate, not a payslip — actual deductions depend on your specific employment contract, nationality, and any active loans or advances. Confirm your exact structure with your employer's HR department.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my basic salary matter if my take-home pay is the same?

Because UAE end-of-service gratuity is calculated from basic salary, not gross salary — a lower basic-to-gross ratio quietly reduces your eventual gratuity payout even though your monthly take-home pay is unaffected.

How is UAE take-home pay calculated?

Gross monthly salary minus any monthly deductions such as loan repayments, pension contributions for UAE/GCC nationals, or salary advances.

What counts as an allowance in a UAE salary structure?

Anything in your gross salary beyond your basic salary — commonly housing, transport, and other allowances.

What is a good basic-to-gross ratio in the UAE?

There's no legal minimum, but a higher basic-to-gross ratio means a larger gratuity and overtime base, since both are calculated on basic salary. A lower ratio (more of your pay as allowances) doesn't change your monthly take-home, but it does reduce your gratuity accrual over time.
