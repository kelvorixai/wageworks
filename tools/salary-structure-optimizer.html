Why the split matters even though your take-home doesn't change

Our free take-home pay calculator and basic vs gross explainer both cover what lands in your account each month — which is identical whatever the split. This workbook goes further: gratuity and overtime pay are calculated on basic salary only, so a lower basic percentage can quietly cost you tens of thousands of dirhams over a multi-year contract, on paper-identical pay.
