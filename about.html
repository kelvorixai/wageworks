About WageWorks

A small library of plain-English, data-driven guides on how UAE salary, gratuity, and take-home pay actually work — the mechanics behind the payslip.

Most salary content online is written to help you negotiate a number. WageWorks deliberately isn't that. Every guide here breaks down the mechanics behind a payslip or benefit calculation — how gratuity is actually formula-derived, how basic and gross and take-home pay differ — using the underlying rules and methodology, not advice on what to ask for.

Everything here is general education, not personalized financial, tax, or HR advice, and not tied to any employer, bank, or government body. Figures are illustrative and based on general UAE labour law and market practice at the time of writing — real entitlements vary by contract and employer. For a number specific to your situation, confirm directly with your employer's HR department or a licensed professional.
